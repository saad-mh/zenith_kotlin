# Changelog

## 2.0

The first release of the native Android app.

### What it does
- Expenses, income, money owed both ways and settlements, with arithmetic entry and repeat
- Splitting an expense equally, by items or by custom amounts
- Accounts, categories with icons, and a default category per kind (fixed or last used)
- Monthly budgets with a gauge, per-category tiles and an even-pace marker
- Insights: cash flow, spend per day, six-month trend and category share
- People: running balances, history, reminders and a shareable statement, optionally bolded for WhatsApp
- CSV and JSON export, encrypted backup and restore
- Light, dark and monochrome themes; large-font layouts
- Offline: no account and no internet permission

### Notes
- Requires Android 12 or newer.
- Not an upgrade of the earlier Zenith app, and not signed with its key: export from the old app before removing it.
- Builds installed from source are signed differently from the release. Back up inside the app before
  switching, because Android requires an uninstall in between.
